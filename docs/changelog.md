# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **Pledges: MSRP and what you saved.** A new *MSRP* column on Pledges and the Melt calculator shows the ships' store price today next to the melt value (what you paid), with *saved $X (N%)* under it — or *$X over* when the pledge cost more — and sorts by the saving. An upgraded pledge is priced at the ship the CCUs took it to, so a Carrack reached from a $45 Aurora through cheap CCUs shows what the chain saved. A *saved vs MSRP* tile adds it up over the pledges on show. Ships only: pledges with no ship, a ship the store doesn't price (Concierge-only, retired), or no dollar value (gifted, bought in game) show —; game packages and paints aren't priced, so a game package can read as *over*.
- **Group by ship: clearer badges and a Show filter.** The ship badge said *hull* / *no hull*; it now says *you own it* (or *you own 2*) and *ship not owned · only its paints / parts / CCUs* (grey, not a warning). A *Show* switch above the groups picks all, ships you own, ships not owned, or pledges not tied to any ship (gear, flair, game packages, unmatched items); remembered. The columns now line up from one ship to the next (every group's table uses the same column widths).

## 0.9.0 — 2026-10-31
- **What's in your RSI cart already.** Browse, Ships (paints by ship), Store changes, Buyback and the CCU planner read your RSI cart back when they open and after every add: lines already in it show *in cart ×n*, and the first click on such a line only asks *Add another?* — a second click adds, so a duplicate is a choice, not an accident. *Open RSI cart* shows the cart's item count.
- **Friends: orgs load as you scroll, requests fold away.** A friend row scrolled into view fetches its orgs on its own (missing or expired only, ten handles a batch, ~1 citizen page/s) — no need to press *Load all orgs* for the people you are looking at; the cache now lasts 7 days instead of 24 h since org membership rarely changes (*Load all orgs* still re-reads anything older). *Incoming* and *Sent* friend requests are foldable sections (remembered), so an empty request block no longer takes the top of the page.
- **Pack pieces the hangar page names only halfway now count as owned.** RSI cuts long item titles short on the hangar page ("Lightning Bolt Co. Atzkav 'Starwalker' Sniper R..."), so Browse showed a pack you own as 22 of 23 in your hangar and the Store changes page missed the *owned ×n* tag. A cut-short title now matches a store title that starts with it (Browse, Store changes, and the hangar paint → store paint link on Ships).
- **Settings → Debug: export a debug file.** *Export debug file* saves `rsi-plus-debug-YYYY-MM-DD.json` with everything RSI+ keeps in this browser, plus extension version, browser and number format, for bug reports. Pledges now keep RSI's raw value, currency and flag fields as read, so a wrong number can be traced to the text behind it (reload your hangar once to fill them in). Sign-in tokens, cookies and keys are never in the file; your handle, friends' handles (also where they key a list, as `#1`, `#2` …), saved-login ids, avatars, muted authors, ship names you gave and notes are written as `[masked]` unless *Include names* is on. RSI's public store data (catalog, paints, listings, store log), most of the file, is left out unless *Include store data* is on. Nothing is sent: you share the file yourself.
- **Show amounts in your currency.** Settings → Appearance → *Currency*: USD (default), CAD, EUR, GBP, AUD and more. RSI lists everything in USD, so another currency converts with a rate you type (1 USD = …); nothing is fetched, the amounts are estimates, and exports stay in USD. Amounts are written your browser's way (`900 €` in German, `$14` in Canadian English). The bar on RSI's own pages stays in USD, like RSI.
- **Prices in other formats are read right.** "1.234,56 €" used to read as 1.23; amounts with a comma decimal mark, spaces between thousands, or €, £, CA$ and UEC now read as written.
- **Pledges bought in game no longer count as dollars.** A pledge RSI lists in UEC (bought with aUEC in game, e.g. "¤15,000 UEC") showed as $15,000 and was added to your hangar value. It now has no dollar value; the UEC price is kept separately. Reload your hangar once.
- **Lighter on the GPU.** The hub header, footer and feed day headers, the Paint rebuy bar, dialogs and the Spectrum thread bar no longer blur what is under them (their backgrounds were nearly opaque anyway, dialogs now dim instead); loading panels fade instead of blurring; muted Spectrum threads are faded without a grayscale filter; the feed no longer rebuilds its scroll watcher for every row that comes into view.
- **Snappier hub.** Clicking a page in the sidebar shows it as picked right away and swaps the page in just after, instead of holding the click until the new page was built (up to ~0.8 s with a big hangar). Long lists — Pledges, Melt, Items, Browse, Buyback, Friends, Ship catalog, Ships — now build only the rows near the screen and add more as you scroll (a "… N more below" line marks the end), instead of putting every row in the page: Pledges went from ~17,000 page elements to ~1,700 with a 566-pledge hangar. Opening Pledges also no longer re-matches every item against the ship catalog from scratch (~0.3 s less). Note: the browser's find-in-page only sees rows that have loaded.
- **Wiki links only where the wiki has a page.** The globe next to a ship or item name now shows only when the Star Citizen Wiki has a page (or redirect) by that name, and opens that page directly; it no longer falls back to a wiki search. The list of page titles ships with RSI+ (refreshed with `scripts/wiki-titles.mjs`), so still no request goes to the wiki. A few special editions and most paints have no page of their own and now show no globe.

## 0.8.0 — 2026-10-31
- **StarOrg Tools: see exactly what is sent.** The StarOrg Tools card (Settings → Integrations) has a *What is sent* list, readable before you connect: every field a send carries, in plain words, and what is never sent. *Preview* builds the exact list a send would contain from your saved hangar and shows it (with *Copy JSON*) without sending anything; in screenshare mode your handle, ship names and values are masked on screen. The privacy policy now spells out each request RSI+ makes to StarOrg Tools and every field, field by field.
- **Star Citizen Wiki links.** A small globe now sits next to ship and item names on Ships, Store, Pledges, Buyback, Items, Browse and Your CCUs; it opens that ship's page on the Star Citizen Wiki (starcitizen.tools) in a new tab. Ships go straight to their page by RSI's catalog name; components, FPS weapons, armour and paints open the wiki's search, which lands on the page when there is one. Packages, insurance, hangar flair, credits and CCUs get no globe — the wiki has nothing for them. It is a plain link: RSI+ sends nothing to the wiki, and your custom ship names are never used.
- **Fleet export: ship names land on the ship you named.** In a pack, every ship got the names of all the named ships in it ("Eldritch Singularity, Eldritch Wayfinder" on the Perseus *and* the Polaris). RSI shows each ship's name on that ship, and RSI+ now reads it there, so only the ships you named carry a name, each its own. Reload your hangar once (Hangar → Pledges) so packs pick up the names per ship.
- **CCU planner: saving a chain or a fleet plan no longer changes it.** Saving a chain used to reserve its own owned CCUs against itself, so the planner re-planned without them — a dearer chain or none at all — and the guide dropped back to *Verify every step*. A fleet plan did the same after *Save plan* (totals jumped, and saving again stored the worse chains). The chain on screen and the fleet plan being edited now keep their own CCUs; other saved chains still reserve theirs. *Open* on a fleet goal now shows that goal's chain exactly, without the CCUs the goals above it take — and going back to the fleet plan afterwards leaves it as it was (those CCUs aren't marked as force-excluded in *Your CCUs*).
- **Paint rebuy: no more doubled cart lines.** Leaving Paint rebuy for another page (or reloading the hub) and coming back made the guide offer to add the whole plan again, and RSI added a second copy of every paint. Adding now reads your RSI cart first and adds only what is still short per paint, so lines already there are skipped. *Confirm cart* also flags a paint with more copies than planned as *too many* and won't call the cart safe to melt until the extras are gone.
- **Accounts: *Log out here* no longer mixes up two accounts' data.** Logging out, logging in as someone else and saving filed the first account's hangar, labels, feed and CCU chains under the new one, and switching back to the first account then emptied them. *Log out here* now puts the current account's data away with its saved login and starts the logged-out state clean (the page reloads), so the next account you save starts empty and switching back brings everything back.
- **A stopped hangar load no longer counts as your whole hangar.** If you pressed *Stop* (or the load broke off) part-way through your hangar, Buyback used to mark entries whose items sat on the pages not read yet as *not in hangar* — and *Add all missing to cart* would put things you already own in your RSI cart. Buyback now says *hangar partly loaded (page n)* with a *Resume* link instead of judging ownership, and *My ships → Export fleet* waits until the whole hangar is loaded, so FleetYards and the other tools never get a fleet with ships missing.
- **Buyback: store filter and ship grouping fixed.** The *Only entries the store sells today* switch is now a *Store* menu: any, *Store sells it today*, or *Store doesn't sell it today* — the entries only buy-back can get you back (CCUs and entries whose contents aren't loaded yet match neither, and the count says how many were left out). *Group: ship* put every entry under "No ship" when the store catalog had not been loaded yet (a fresh install, Store pages never opened): the Buyback tab now loads the catalog itself, like My items, and says so if it can't. Both choices are remembered.
- **Buyback: group by type or by ship.** The Buyback tab has a *Group by* menu next to its filters: by type (ships, packages, combos, CCUs, paints, gear, …) or by ship, where each entry sits under the hull it is for — a CCU under the ship it upgrades to, a pack under every ship in it, a paint pack under its hull — with anything naming no ship at the end. Groups fold open and shut, and the choice is remembered.
- **StarOrg Tools and the account switcher are opt-in.** Settings → Integrations has a *StarOrg Tools* switch, off by default: until you turn it on, the StarOrg card and the *JSON for StarOrg Tools* export are hidden and nothing is sent; turning it off again disconnects. The account switcher in the hub header and the popup is now a setting too (*Account switcher*, off by default); if you already saved a login it stays on after the update.
- **Pick the StarOrg Tools site.** The StarOrg card has a *Site* dropdown: production (starorg.tools) or dev (dev.starorg.tools). It is fixed while connected, and the sign-in token only ever goes to the site that issued it. Both sites are optional hosts in every build; only the one you connect to is asked for.
- **Send your hangar to StarOrg Tools in one click (optional, off until you connect).** Settings has a new *Integrations* card. *Connect* asks your browser for permission to reach starorg.tools and signs you in through StarOrg's own Discord sign-in; the card then shows your StarOrg name, your RSI handle there and the last send. *Send hangar now* sends your ship list (hull, nickname, LTI / warbond / insurance, pledge id and pledge name, plus the ships you have only in the game) for the RSI account you are logged in with; pledge values go only with *Send pledge values* on. *Auto-sync* (off by default) sends after every full hangar load, and once a week re-reads your hangar from RSI and sends it if your ships changed (not in passive mode; switching passive mode or Auto-sync off removes the weekly check). Only a hangar read while the same RSI handle was logged in is sent — log into another account on the website and RSI+ asks you to load that hangar first instead of sending the old one under the new name — and switching profiles is never mistaken for a load. Hulls StarOrg doesn't know yet are listed on the card, and a hangar over StarOrg's 500-row limit is caught before sending. If StarOrg has verified a different RSI handle for you, the card names it so you can switch accounts. *Disconnect* signs out on StarOrg and removes the permission (kept while an incognito window is still connected); a cancelled or failed *Connect* gives the permission back. On Firefox, *Send pledge values* asks for the "financial and payment information" data category. Until you connect, RSI+ still talks to nothing but RSI; PRIVACY.md, the README and the store listing say so. New permission: `identity` (for the sign-in window); `starorg.tools` is an optional host, asked for only on *Connect*.
- **Export your fleet to StarOrg Tools.** *My ships → Export fleet* has a new *JSON for StarOrg Tools* entry that downloads `starorg-fleet-YYYY-MM-DD.json` for starorg.tools → Hangar. It holds the same rows as the HangarXPLOR file, without pledge dates, plus each pledge's insurance term and the ships you have only in the game (bought with aUEC or earned), one row per copy. The HangarXPLOR JSON files (My ships and Pledges) now also carry `insurance` when a pledge lists one; FleetYards, myfleet.gg and Starjump ignore the extra key, and the CSV columns are unchanged.
- **Fleet export guarded against catalog drift.** A committed snapshot of RSI's store catalog (`tests/fixtures/catalog.json`, regenerated with `node scripts/dump-catalog.mjs`), plus the concept hulls RSI never lists such as the Kraken and Javelin, now runs through both fleet exports in the tests: every hull must get exactly one row with a ship code of its own, the same code every time, so a renamed or new hull shows up as a failing test instead of a mismatched ship on StarOrg Tools.

  - *Faster*: the hub loads one page's code at a time instead of all fifteen (hub chunk 1.1 MB → 66 KB; the others fetch in idle time). The CCU fleet planner shares one upgrade graph across hulls and goals with a heap-based route (same results, about half the time). Every remaining per-row tooltip mounts on first hover (Friends, Store, Paint rebuy, Feed chips, Segmented hints). - **Wallet → Where the money went.** What the hangar can prove: new money for sure (warbond), cash-or-store-credit (standard pledges), credit you got back by melting (priced buyback entries), gifts and flair, paid customization, and a cash-spent estimate as a range. *Import order history (beta)* reads your billing pages for the real split — cash vs store credit vs gifts vs refunds, by year; *Copy billing markup* when it recognises nothing, so the reader can learn RSI's layout.

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-22
- **Paints by ship: add to cart, and packs you already have piece by piece count as owned.** Every store paint row gets *+ cart* next to the store link (*in cart* once added, *Open RSI cart (n)* in the header; nothing is paid). A paint pack or collection whose every paint is already in your hangar no longer shows as a paint you don't own — its contents come from the Browse listing, so open Browse once if a pack still shows.
- **Browse: paint packs vs singles under RSI's multi-paint discount.** A paint pack's panel adds a line pricing its paints as singles at each tier — these n alone (15 % at 2, 20 % at 3), then in a cart of 5+ (25 %), 7+ (30 %), 10+ (35 %) — and says whether that beats the pack and by how much. The pack line is taken at its listed price; whether RSI discounts a pack line too, or counts it toward the tier, is unverified (tooltip says so).
- **Browse: buy a pack's pieces on their own.** A piece the store sells alone gets its name linked to its RSI page, an ↗ button and a cart button on its tile (hulls: the ship page and the cheapest standalone SKU on sale); *in cart* / *Open RSI cart* work as for rows. Pieces never sold alone have neither.
- **Browse: a pack's contents as tiles, with pictures.** Opening a pack, collection, kit or set lays its pieces out as tiles — picture, name, price (or *included* / *not sold apart*), a green tint and check on what is already in your hangar — hulls and single items first, then each set the pack is priced by (armor sets, paint packs) as its own box with the set's price and its pieces inside. Pictures come from the store itself: hulls from the ship catalog, everything else from the listing that sells it alone or as that set; a piece the store never sells on its own gets a blank tile (RSI doesn't ship pictures with a pack's contents).
- **Fix: a thumbnail's big preview could stay open after the pointer left.** The preview is set up on the first hover, and when the pointer had already moved on by then nothing closed it; it now closes whenever the pointer leaves the thumbnail.
- **Browse: packs say how much of them you already own.** A pack's *Contents* cell says *3 of 7 in your hangar* (or *all 7 in your hangar*) under *n items* — the count leaves out the hangar, download and UEC extras that *n items* includes, which the tooltip explains — and the opened panel adds *In your hangar 3 of 7* with *Missing apart $X* — what the pieces you don't have would cost one by one (a set with a piece missing counts as the whole set; pieces never sold alone are counted). Duplicates in the pack count as separate pieces; hangar, download and UEC extras don't count. The *Hangar* select gains *Pieces: all owned / some owned / none owned* — by a pack's parts rather than the pack itself (a collection whose paints you bought one by one is *all owned*; *some owned* is only ever a pack), and the hangar column sorts by share owned.
- **Fix: dismissing a notification could corrupt the log** ("r.entries is not iterable" on the hub). Dismiss handed the page's reactive copy of the log to chrome.storage, which saves such objects as `{}`; it now saves a plain snapshot, and malformed groups already saved are skipped. Counts read as *+32* (green) / *−5* (red) — on the sidebar entry, the card titles, the type headers and the Added / Removed filters.
- **Notifications: store changes on their own sidebar page, with pictures and dismiss.** *Store changes* left the top of Browse for a *Notifications* entry in the sidebar (Store group; also in the popup) that carries a count of groups still to look at. Each group is split by type (ships, CCUs, paints, packs, gear …) with bigger tiles, 12 per type before *+n more*. A poll's catalog check (ships, CCUs) and listing check (paints, packs, gear …) used to show as two *Added* groups; they are one card now (same action within 10 minutes), and the count on the sidebar counts cards. Tiles of things already in your hangar are tinted green with an *owned ×n* badge (standalone hulls and items; a CCU says *hull owned* when you have its target). Tiles carry *Warbond* and *LTI* / *n mo insurance* tags, and a pack's *n items* chip lists what is inside on hover. Added items and standalone hulls have a cart button (*Open RSI cart (n)* appears in the header); a CCU needs a ship to start from, so its button opens the CCU planner on that hull.
- **The fan notice footer only shows on the Feed page (and on mobile).** The sidebar footer already carries it on every page; the Fankit asks for an always-visible nav area and/or the home page, not both. Each entry is a tile with the store picture (hover for the big one), kind and price; ✕ on a tile dismisses that entry, ✕ on a type header (Ships, Paints …) dismisses that type, ✕ on a group dismisses the group, *Dismiss all* (or *Dismiss added / removed* under those filters) clears the list — dismissed means gone from the log.
- **Store changes: every entry links to where it lives in RSI+, with an RSI button beside it.** Clicking a line shows it in the extension — a hull on the Ship catalog (every hull, search set to it, row highlighted), an item on Browse (filters cleared, search set to it) — and the ↗ button opens the RSI store page (the hull's page for CCUs and standalones, the listing's own page for paints, packs, gear …). Browse also moves the contents toggle to the far left of the row, like Pledges, with the price, discount and cart right after the contents instead of across the page.
